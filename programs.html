Program 01 · Options, with assumptions

Strategy and planning

A useful plan is a set of costed options with their assumptions written separately, so you can argue with the assumptions rather than accept the conclusion. We will also say when the honest answer is that a business is not ready for the thing it wants to do, which is not what most people are hoping to hear from a strategy session.

Program 02 · The real process

Operations and process

The written process and the real one differ almost everywhere, and the real one is where the delays live. We reconstruct it with the people doing the work rather than from the org chart, and we look first for steps to remove. Adding a system on top of a process nobody questioned just makes the problem permanent.

Program 03 · Materials you keep

Training and workshops

Training that produces enthusiasm and no change is the norm, so sessions end with something written down that someone owns and a date it will be reviewed. We would rather cover three things that get implemented than twelve that get remembered fondly. Materials stay licensed for your own use afterwards.

Program 04 · And what we refer

Ongoing advisory support

Regular support is where an unregulated adviser drifts into territory that needs authorisation, usually with good intentions. Investment, pensions and financial planning need FCA authorisation. Reserved legal activities need a regulated lawyer. Accounts, tax and audit need the relevant professional. We refer those rather than offering a view in the margins.
